🎁 Prize Wheel

Spin up a fair winner, every time

Prize Wheel

Giveaways live or die on trust. If people suspect the winner was picked before the stream started, the prize stops being fun. This prize wheel makes the draw visible and honest: everything is on screen, the spin is real, and the winner is whoever the pointer lands on. Use it for stream giveaways, classroom rewards, office raffles or party prize draws.

What's on the wheel

Twelve prize-style results to get you going — first place through to a mystery prize, plus a few fun blanks and a "spin again" to build suspense. Swap in your actual prize names or your entrants' names in one click and you're running a real draw.

How to use the Prize Wheel

  • Load your entries. Hit “Customize this wheel” and paste in every name or ticket number, then share the wheel link with your audience so they can see the list.
  • Draw in order. Turn on Remove winner after spin to pick first, second and third place without anyone winning twice.
  • Spin in public. Do it live or on a recording — a visible spin is the strongest proof of fairness you can offer.
  • Announce with confetti. The wheel fires a confetti burst on the result, which makes the reveal feel like an event.

Make it yours

Want to add, remove or reword the options? Hit “Customize this wheel” under the spinner — it opens this exact list in the full wheel editor, where you can edit entries, change the theme, and share your version with a link. You can also toggle Remove winner after spin to run through every option without repeats.

FAQ

Is the prize wheel fair?
Yes — each entry occupies an equal segment and the winner is determined by where the wheel stops, not by any preset order. Spinning in front of your audience makes it visibly fair.

Can I add all my giveaway entries?
Yes. Click “Customize this wheel”, paste in your list of names or entries, and spin. You can also enable “Remove winner after spin” to draw multiple winners without repeats.

Can I use it for a live stream giveaway?
Absolutely — share it on screen and spin live. Because the segments and result are all visible, viewers can see the draw was honest.

Does it cost anything?
No. The prize wheel is completely free with no sign-up, no limits on spins, and no watermark to cover up.
