🎄 Christmas Movies

Festive film night, decided in one spin

Christmas Movies Wheel

It's December, the tree's up, and everyone wants to watch a Christmas film — just not the same one. Rather than a twenty-minute negotiation over hot chocolate, let the wheel settle it. Spin, grab a blanket, and let the holiday movie marathon begin.

What's on the wheel

A festive spread of Christmas classics, animated family favourites, romantic comedies, and a couple of modern picks. Enough variety to suit a cosy solo night, a family gathering or a movie marathon with friends.

How to use this wheel

  • Spin for the first film: Whoever's hosting spins, and the result starts the marathon. No rewinds, no “but we watched that last year”.
  • Build a marathon: Turn on Remove winner after spin and keep spinning to line up a whole evening of films with no repeats.
  • Match the audience: Watching with kids? Customize the wheel to leave in only the family-friendly picks. Adult night? Keep the rom-coms and classics.
  • Save your favourites: Use “Customize this wheel” to load your actual shelf of festive films and share the link with the group.

Make it yours

Want to add, remove or reword the options? Hit “Customize this wheel” under the spinner — it opens this exact list in the full wheel editor, where you can edit entries, change the theme, and share your version with a link. You can also toggle Remove winner after spin to run through every option without repeats.

FAQ

Is Die Hard really a Christmas movie?
That debate is exactly why it's on the wheel, and exactly why the wheel settles it. If your group disagrees, spin — the wheel's ruling is final.

Can I add my own Christmas films?
Yes — “Customize this wheel” opens the editor so you can swap in your family's must-watch list, then share the link so everyone sees the same films.

Can I use it for a whole month of films?
Turn on “Remove winner after spin” and you'll work through the entire list once, giving you a December viewing calendar with no repeats.

Does it work for other holidays?
Of course — the wheel is just a list. Open the editor and rename the entries for Halloween, Thanksgiving or any other seasonal marathon.
